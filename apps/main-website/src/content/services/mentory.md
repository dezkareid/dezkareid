## Who this is for

Software developers at any stage — from mid-level engineers trying to break into senior roles, to senior engineers navigating the move into staff or leadership. Also for developers who feel stuck, unsure of which skills to invest in, or preparing for interviews at competitive companies.

## Service Overview

I've navigated the same decisions you're facing and made the mistakes so you don't have to. I cut through the noise of "learn everything" advice and help you build a focused plan based on where you actually want to go.

Sessions cover whatever is most useful: career strategy, code review, system design practice, technical communication, or preparing for specific interview loops.

### How it works

- We schedule a call to talk about your career goals and where you are today.
- We define a plan: concrete actions, deadlines, and metrics to track your progress.
- I run mock interviews and simulated real-world situations — code reviews, system design, leadership, and feedback conversations.
- We iterate on the plan based on your progress and changing priorities.
