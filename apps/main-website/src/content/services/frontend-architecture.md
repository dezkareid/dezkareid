## Who this is for

Engineering teams that are shipping slower than they should, accumulating frontend debt faster than they can pay it down, or about to make a significant architectural decision and want an independent perspective before committing.

## Service Overview

A poorly structured frontend compounds over time — slow builds, hard-to-test components, inconsistent patterns, and new features that take three times longer than they should. I audit your current setup, identify the root causes of friction, and design a concrete path forward.

The deliverable is a prioritized, actionable document your team can act on immediately — not a generic best-practices checklist.

### How it works

- I start by understanding your business goals and the current state of your application.
- I analyze your codebase, workflows, tooling, and team structure.
- I deliver findings and prioritized recommendations in clear, actionable documents.
- Optionally, I can implement the solutions or train your team to do it themselves.
